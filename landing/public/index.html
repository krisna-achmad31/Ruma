Questions people ask.

Does my partner have to pay too?

No. One Plus subscription automatically covers both of you in the same home.

How does the invite code work?

One of you creates a home in Ruma and gets a 6-character code. The other installs Ruma, taps "I have an invite code", and enters it. You are now in the same home, and everything syncs in real time.

Do I have to connect a bank account?

No. Ruma never asks for bank logins. You log transactions yourself with quick entry, so you decide what goes in.

Is our financial data safe?

Your home's data can only be read by members of that home. Important numbers and documents go in a vault locked with a PIN or your fingerprint.

Does it work on iPhone?

Ruma launches on Android first. The iPhone version is next on the roadmap.
