Privacy policy

Effective 6 October 2026

Ruma is a home app for couples. You trust us with chores, money, and personal moments, so we keep this simple: we collect what the app needs to work for your home, we do not sell it, and you can ask us to delete it.

Who we are. Ruma is operated by [operator name], [address] ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy go to [contact email].

1. What we collect

Account data. Your name, email address, and sign-in details when you register with email or with Google. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.

Home data you enter. Everything you add to your shared home, for example:

  • Chores and reminders, including who remembered and who did them, and thank-you messages
  • Money records: wallets, envelopes and budgets, transactions, transfers, installments and paylater, savings goals, assets, and monthly reports
  • Check-ins (mood and energy), conversation cards, journal entries, and timeline moments
  • Calendar events, home maintenance items, shopping lists, and life stage plans such as a wedding, a baby, or Eid
  • Photos you choose to attach, stored as small compressed images
  • Items you save in the home vault, such as important numbers, policies, and warranties
  • Your home's name, the city you enter for weather, and the invite code that links you and your partner

Device data. Your language preference and some settings are stored only on your phone. If you lock the vault with a fingerprint or face, that check happens on your device through the operating system. We never receive your biometric data.

We do not access your bank accounts, e-wallets, contacts, or precise location, and the app does not contain advertising or third-party analytics.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: show your home's data to both partners, sync it in real time, and send in-app notifications such as reminders and thank-yous
  • To calculate things for you, like how much is safe to spend today
  • To manage your Plus subscription when payments are enabled
  • To answer support requests and keep the service secure

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your home data to build advertising profiles.

3. Who can see your data

Your home. Data in a home can only be read and changed by the members of that home, enforced by our database security rules. When your partner joins with your invite code, they can see the home's shared data. That is the point of Ruma, so only share your code with your partner.

Service providers. We rely on a small number of providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store your account and home data. Data may be stored on Google servers outside Indonesia.
  • Google Play to process subscription payments. We receive the status of your subscription, not your card details.
  • Open-Meteo to show the weather. The app sends only the city name you entered, never your account or home data.

Legal reasons. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account data and your personal entries within 30 days. Shared home data that your partner still uses stays in the home until the home is deleted. Backups held by our providers are removed on their regular schedule.

5. Your rights

Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) and similar laws where you live, you can:

  • Access and get a copy of your data
  • Correct data that is wrong, most of which you can edit directly in the app
  • Delete your account and data
  • Withdraw consent and stop using the app at any time

To delete your account or make any other request, email [contact email] from the email address on your account. We reply within 14 days.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Firebase. Access is limited to members of each home. The vault can be locked with a PIN or your device's biometrics. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

7. Children

Ruma is meant for adults managing a home. Accounts are for users aged 18 and over. Information about children that parents enter, such as checkup schedules, is part of the parents' home data.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app before they take effect.
